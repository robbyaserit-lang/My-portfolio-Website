02 ABOUT & ETHOS

Less, but better.

Hello, I am Robi. I am an engineer and interface craftsman operating at the cross-section of systematic engineering and minimalist design aesthetics.

I believe modern software often suffers from visual excess and needless complexity. My practice is centered on stripping away superficial decoration until only what is vital, fast, and remarkably intuitive remains.

Over the last five years, I have architected web systems for high-growth startups, open-source maintainers, and design ateliers. I obsess over the 100ms response window, typographic hierarchy, keyboard navigation, and semantic HTML that stands the test of time.

Core Operating Principles

01

Radical Simplicity

Every element on the screen must fight for its right to exist. If it doesn't clarify the user's intent or accelerate the outcome, it is deleted.

02

Speed as a Design Pillar

Performance isn't an engineering afterthought—it is the cornerstone of respect for the user's time. Zero lag, instant feedback.

03

Accessible by Default

A website that isn't accessible to all users is fundamentally broken. Strict keyboard flow, semantic hierarchy, and contrast ratios are mandatory.
